# Industrial Implementation Case Studies

## 1. Automotive Engine Support Bracket
* **Application:** Engine mounting bracket (Millat Tractors application)[cite: 3].
* **Material & Batch:** ABS, 150 g component mass, pilot batch of 20 units[cite: 3].
* **Functional Requirements:** Surface roughness $R_a < 0.6\,\mu\text{m}$ for fluid drainage, tensile capacity retention, dimensional tolerance within $\pm 0.3\,\text{mm}$[cite: 3].
* **Selected Regimen:** Treatment E (Sanding $\rightarrow$ Saturated Vapor Smoothing $\rightarrow$ Epoxy Sealant)[cite: 3].
* **Unit Production Cost:** PKR 180 (~$0.65 USD) scaled[cite: 3].

## 2. Automated Sorting End-Effector Gripper
* **Application:** Gripper jaw for municipal waste separation robotic cell[cite: 3].
* **Material & Batch:** PLA, 45 g, single functional prototype[cite: 3].
* **Functional Requirements:** Reduced surface friction, fast fabrication turnaround, zero aggressive chemical exposure[cite: 3].
* **Selected Regimen:** Treatment A (Progressive 400 $\rightarrow$ 600 $\rightarrow$ 1000 Grit Sanding)[cite: 2, 3].
* **Unit Production Cost:** PKR 30 (~$0.10 USD)[cite: 3].

## 3. Commercial Electronic Device Enclosure
* **Application:** Consumer electronics housing aesthetic display[cite: 3].
* **Material & Batch:** ABS, 200 g component mass, batch of 10 units[cite: 3].
* **Functional Requirements:** High gloss aesthetic finish, lightweight build, no extended thermal cure cycles[cite: 3].
* **Selected Regimen:** Treatment D (Abrasive Sanding + Acetone Vapor Smoothing)[cite: 2, 3].
* **Unit Production Cost:** PKR 120 (~$0.43 USD)[cite: 3].
