DETAILED METHODOLOGY & EXPERIMENTAL PROCEDURES
Post-Processing Techniques for FDM 3D-Printed Parts
1. EQUIPMENT & MATERIALS
1.1 FDM Printer Specifications
• Model: Creality Ender-3 Pro (or equivalent)
• Build Plate: 220 × 220 mm, heated (up to 100°C for ABS)
• Nozzle Diameter: 0.4 mm (standard)
• Maximum Temperature: 250°C (nozzle), 100°C (bed)
• Z-axis Calibration: ±0.1 mm before printing each batch
1.2 Post-Processing Equipment
Sanding Station:
  - Sandpaper: 400, 600, 1000 grit (3M brand, 3×4" sheets)
  - Backing Block: 3×3" wooden block with rubber facing
  - Dust Collection: Bench vacuum with HEPA filter
Vapor Smoothing Station (Fume Hood):
  - Chemical: Acetone (ACS grade, ≥99.5%) for ABS; Ethyl Acetate for PLA
  - Container: 500 mL borosilicate glass jar with hermetic seal
  - Suspension: Metal grid or fishing line to suspend parts above liquid
  - Timer: Digital stopwatch (±0.1 s accuracy)
Coating Station:
  - Epoxy Resin: Two-part epoxy (Araldite or equivalent) with 5:1 ratio
  - Application: Small synthetic brush (1 inch) or foam applicator
  - Curing: 24 hours at 25°C, relative humidity 45–55%
1.3 Measurement Equipment
Surface Profilometer:
  - Model: Mitutoyo SJ-410 (or Mahr Perthometer M1)
  - Measurement Length: 4.8 mm (12 × cutoff)
  - Parameter: Ra (arithmetic mean roughness) per ISO 4287
  - Calibration: Verify with standard reference sample before each session
Dimensional Measurement:
  - Calipers: Mitutoyo digital caliper, ±0.05 mm accuracy
  - Measurement Points: Length (3 points), Width (3 points), Thickness (5 points)
Tensile Testing Machine:
  - Equipment: Universal Testing Machine (Tinius Olsen or equivalent)
  - Capacity: 0–5 kN load cell (±0.5% accuracy)
  - Grip Type: Pneumatic grips with serrated faces
  - Speed: 5 mm/min crosshead speed (ASTM D638)
2. SAMPLE PREPARATION PROTOCOL
2.1 Print Settings (All Samples)
Layer Height: 0.2 mm
Infill Density: 100% (solid parts for mechanical testing)
Print Speed: 50 mm/s
Nozzle Temperature – ABS: 240°C | PLA: 200°C
Bed Temperature – ABS: 80°C | PLA: 50°C
Support: NONE (parts designed without overhangs)
Raft/Brim: None (direct plate adhesion with Boxymod bed texture)
2.2 Post-Print Cooling
• Cool to room temperature (25°C) before removing from build plate
• Cooling time: 30 minutes (natural air cooling)
• Remove support material with craft knife (deburr if needed)
• Clean with dry cloth; store in sealed container at 25°C for 24 hours before processing
3. POST-PROCESSING PROCEDURES (DETAILED STEP-BY-STEP)
3.1 Treatment A: SANDING ONLY
Equipment Setup:
1. Set up dust collection system and activate
2. Mount 400-grit sandpaper on backing block using spray adhesive
3. Secure sample in vise or clamp (protect with rubber pads)
Sanding Process:
Stage 1 (400-grit): 5 minutes of circular hand motion, light to medium pressure (~1 kg force). Sand all exposed surfaces, including top and side edges. Do NOT sand base (bottom) surface.
• Visual Check: Matte finish should appear uniform; original layer lines becoming less pronounced
Stage 2 (600-grit): Replace sandpaper. Sand for 3 minutes with medium pressure. Focus on any remaining rough areas. Sand with cross-hatch pattern to maintain uniformity.
• Visual Check: Surface should appear increasingly smooth and even
Stage 3 (1000-grit): Replace sandpaper. Sand for 2 minutes with light pressure. Sand with circular motions to achieve smooth finish.
• Visual Check: Part should have satin finish; high gloss is NOT expected
Post-Sanding:
• Wipe clean with damp microfiber cloth to remove dust
• Air dry for 15 minutes
• Store in sealed container; ready for testing
3.2 Treatment B: VAPOR SMOOTHING ONLY
Safety Precautions:
⚠️ Perform in FUME HOOD with full ventilation
⚠️ Wear nitrile gloves and face shield
⚠️ No open flames; use with electrical safety precautions
Chamber Setup:
1. Pour 150 mL acetone (ABS) or ethyl acetate (PLA) into glass jar
2. Install metal grid ~3 cm above liquid surface
3. Place jar in fume hood; allow solvent vapors to accumulate for 2 minutes
Smoothing Process:
For ABS:
  • Place sample on grid (do NOT touch liquid)
  • Hermetically seal jar (use plastic wrap + rubber band as backup)
  • Expose for exactly 8 minutes
  • Timer Start: When lid is sealed
For PLA:
  • Use ethyl acetate (acetone damages PLA)
  • Expose for 10 minutes (slower dissolution of PLA)
Post-Exposure:
• Immediately remove part and place in open air under fume hood
• Allow solvent to evaporate (5–10 minutes); surface should be glossy
• Dispose of used solvent as hazardous waste per local regulations
• Allow part to cool to room temperature before storage
3.3 Treatment C: EPOXY COATING ONLY
Preparation:
1. Lightly sand part with 400-grit paper (2 minutes) to improve adhesion
2. Wipe with damp cloth to remove dust; air dry 10 minutes
3. Place part on wax paper or release sheet
Epoxy Mixing & Application:
• Mix epoxy resin and hardener in 5:1 ratio by weight (use small scale for accuracy)
• Stir thoroughly for 1 minute (avoid incorporating air bubbles)
• Allow to rest for 30 seconds; skim any surface bubbles
• Apply thin, even coat using foam brush or applicator (avoid drips)
• Target thickness: ~150 μm (≈0.15 mm); build with 2 thin coats if needed
Curing:
• Room temperature (23–25°C) for 24 hours minimum
• Humidity 45–55% (excessive humidity causes blooming/cloudiness)
• Post-cure: Heat to 60°C for 2 hours in oven (optional, improves hardness 10%)
Quality Check:
• Inspect for drips, uneven coverage, or bubbles (acceptable: <2 small bubbles)
• Test hardness: Fingernail scratch should NOT leave visible mark
3.4 Treatment D: SANDING + VAPOR SMOOTHING
Sequence:
1. Perform TREATMENT A (Sanding) – all three grit stages (10 min total)
2. Clean thoroughly with damp cloth and air dry (15 min)
3. Perform TREATMENT B (Vapor Smoothing) – see section 3.2
3.5 Treatment E: FULL COMBINED (SANDING + VAPOR + EPOXY)
Sequence:
1. Perform TREATMENT A (Sanding) – sections 3.1 (10 min)
2. Clean and air dry (15 min)
3. Perform TREATMENT B (Vapor Smoothing) – section 3.2 (8–10 min exposure)
4. Cool to room temperature (10 min)
5. Perform TREATMENT C (Epoxy Coating) – section 3.3 (2 coats, 24 hr cure)

4. MEASUREMENT PROCEDURES
4.1 Surface Roughness Measurement (Ra – ISO 4287)
Calibration (perform before each measurement session):
1. Mount reference sample (Mitutoyo SJ standard) on measuring surface
2. Run profilometer scan; recorded value should match reference (within ±5%)
3. If out of tolerance, recalibrate probe or replace if damaged
Measurement Procedure:
1. Mount sample on flat stable surface (use vise with soft jaws or magnetic base)
2. Select top surface as primary measurement location (most affected by post-processing)
3. Perform 5 measurements per sample, at different locations (avoid edges by >5mm)
4. For each measurement: Measurement Length = 4.8 mm (12 × 0.4 mm cutoff)
5. Record Ra value (arithmetic mean roughness) in micrometers (μm)
6. Calculate mean and standard deviation for the 5 measurements
Data Recording:
Report: Mean Ra ± SD (e.g., "2.1 ± 0.3 μm")
4.2 Dimensional Accuracy
Equipment Calibration:
• Digital calipers: Verify zero reading on closed jaws
• Check with calibration block if available (25.0 mm standard)
Measurement Points:
Sample Geometry: 75 mm (length) × 25 mm (width) × 3 mm (thickness)
Length (75 mm nominal):
  - Measure at 3 locations: start, middle, end (avoid edges by 5 mm)
  - Record all 3 values; calculate mean deviation from 75.0 mm nominal
Width (25 mm nominal):
  - Measure at 3 locations along length (start, middle, end)
  - Record all 3 values; calculate mean deviation from 25.0 mm nominal
Thickness (3 mm nominal):
  - Measure at 5 locations (distributed across surface)
  - Record all 5 values; calculate mean deviation from 3.0 mm nominal
Data Reporting:
Report: Dimension ± tolerance deviation (e.g., "75.0 ± 0.28 mm" or "+0.1 / -0.2 mm tolerance")
4.3 Tensile Strength Testing (ASTM D638)
Sample Preparation:
• Print tensile test specimens (Type V per ASTM D638): 25 mm gauge length, 6 mm width, 3 mm thickness
• Process identically to regular samples (same print settings)
• Apply treatments before tensile testing
Testing Procedure:
1. Measure gauge section dimensions precisely (width and thickness with calipers, 3 measurements each)
2. Mount sample in pneumatic grips (50 mm grip distance)
3. Set crosshead speed to 5 mm/min per ASTM D638
4. Start test; record load-extension curve until specimen breaks
5. Note maximum load at break (in Newtons)
Calculation:
Tensile Stress = Maximum Load (N) / Cross-sectional Area (mm²)
Cross-sectional Area = Average Width × Average Thickness (in mm²)
Example: Load = 210 N, Width = 6.0 mm, Thickness = 3.0 mm
  → Area = 6.0 × 3.0 = 18.0 mm²
  → Stress = 210 / 18.0 = 11.7 MPa
Data Reporting (N ≥ 5 per treatment):
Report: Mean Tensile Stress ± SD (e.g., "35.2 ± 2.1 MPa")
5. QUALITY CONTROL & DATA INTEGRITY
• All equipment calibrated weekly; calibration records maintained
• Ambient conditions (23–25°C, 40–55% RH) monitored and logged
• Operator training: All personnel follow SOP exactly; no deviations
• Blind data verification: 10% of samples re-tested by independent operator
• Outlier detection: Data points >2 SD from group mean flagged for review
• All data recorded digitally in spreadsheet with timestamp and operator ID
